08. Useful Pronunciation Tip

Read each Korean word slowly. Try to recognise the Hangul letters before reading the English pronunciation.

안녕하세요 – Annyeonghaseyo – Hello

감사합니다 – Gamsahamnida – Thank you

친구 – Chingu – Friend
