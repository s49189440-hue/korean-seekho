Introduction to Hangul (한글)

1. What is Hangul?

Hangul is the Korean writing system. It is used to read and write the Korean language.

2. History of Hangul

Hangul was created under King Sejong the Great in the 15th century. It was introduced in 1446 to make reading and writing more accessible.
