한글 – Hangul

Discover the Korean Alphabet and Learn How to Read and Write Korean.

📖 What is Hangul?

Hangul is the official writing system of the Korean language. It is known for its logical structure and easy-to-learn design.

Hangul letters are combined into syllable blocks. Each block usually contains a consonant and a vowel, and sometimes a final consonant.

👑 A Short History of Hangul

Hangul was created under the leadership of King Sejong the Great, the fourth king of the Joseon Dynasty.

Before Hangul, many Koreans used Classical Chinese characters for writing. These were difficult for ordinary people to learn.

King Sejong wanted everyone to have a writing system they could learn more easily.

  • 1443: Hangul was created.
  • 1446: Hunminjeongeum was published.
  • October 9: Hangul Day is celebrated in South Korea.

✏️ How to Make Korean Syllables

Korean letters are joined together to form syllable blocks.

ㄱ + ㅏ= 가 (ga)
ㄴ + ㅏ= 나 (na)
ㄷ + ㅏ= 다 (da)
ㅁ + ㅏ= 마 (ma)

Practice reading: 가 나 다 라 마 바 사 아 자 차
